Product Overview:
  Overview: The Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra is Samsung's flagship Android smartphone, built around a titanium frame and a flat 6.8-inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X display with a 120Hz adaptive refresh rate.
  Performance: Powered by Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 for Galaxy and 12GB RAM, the device delivers smooth multitasking, gaming, and AI processing. Designed for demanding applications such as video editing, programming, and high-end mobile gaming.
  Camera: Built around a 200MP main sensor and a 50MP periscope telephoto lens, the camera system is designed for detailed photography, long-range zoom, and low-light shooting, making it a strong choice for photography and content creation.
  Battery: A 5000mAh battery paired with 45W wired and 15W wireless charging is designed for all-day heavy use, covering long workdays, travel, and extended gaming sessions without a mid-day recharge.
  Software: Running Android 14 with One UI 6.1 and Galaxy AI, the software is built around productivity and search, offering Circle to Search, Live Translate, and Chat Assist for everyday tasks and multilingual communication.
